Korean skincare for dry skin: what works, and what Koreans pick

Hwahae lets users register their skin type, so its rankings show what people with dry skin actually rate highly. Here is how to build a routine for dry skin, and today's top 5.

How to build the routine

Layer hydration: a hydrating toner (patted in two or three times), a serum with hyaluronic acid or panthenol, then a ceramide cream. Look for the review tags Moisturizing and Fixes inner dryness.

Ingredients to look for

Ceramides, panthenol, glycerin, squalane.

What to avoid

Foaming cleansers that leave skin tight, and alcohol-heavy toners.
