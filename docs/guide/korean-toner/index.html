Best Korean toner: what Koreans pick, and how to choose

In Korea a toner is not an astringent: it is the first hydrating step after cleansing. Here is how to pick one, and the five toners Korean Hwahae users rank highest today.

Three kinds of Korean toner

  • Hydrating toner (스킨/토너): watery, applied with hands or a cotton pad right after cleansing.
  • Toner pad (토너패드): pre-soaked pads, often with mild exfoliating acids (PHA, LHA) or soothing extracts. Good for a quick routine.
  • Essence toner: thicker, sits between toner and serum. Useful for dry skin.

How to choose

Dry skin: look for glycerin, panthenol or hyaluronic acid. Oily or acne-prone skin: lighter toners or pads with PHA/BHA, used a few times a week. Sensitive skin: short ingredient lists, mugwort (쑥) or centella (병풀). Check the review tags under each product: Korean users often mention Soothing, Moisturizing or Non-sticky.
