Best Korean moisturizer: what Koreans pick, and how to choose

The cream is the step that seals in the toner and serum. Here is how to choose by skin type, and the five creams Korean Hwahae users rank highest today.

Choose by texture

  • Gel cream: light, good for oily skin and hot weather.
  • Barrier cream with ceramides: for dry or irritated skin, and for winter.
  • Soothing cream with centella or panthenol: for redness and sensitive skin.

For skin-type rankings, see dry skin, oily skin and sensitive skin.
