Korean skincare for sensitive skin: what works, and what Koreans pick

Hwahae lets users register their skin type, so its rankings show what people with sensitive skin actually rate highly. Here is how to build a routine for sensitive skin, and today's top 5.

How to build the routine

Fewer products, shorter ingredient lists, patch test first. Korean brands make many soothing lines with centella (병풀) or mugwort (쑥). Mineral sunscreens (무기자차) are usually gentler.

Ingredients to look for

Centella, mugwort, panthenol, madecassoside.

What to avoid

Fragrance, essential oils, strong acids and high-dose vitamin C.
