Best Korean cleanser: what Koreans pick, and how to double cleanse

Koreans usually cleanse twice at night: an oil or balm to remove sunscreen and makeup, then a water-based cleanser. Here is how, and the five cleansers Korean Hwahae users rank highest today.

Double cleansing in two steps

  1. Oil, balm or cleansing water (클렌징 오일/밤/워터) on dry skin, then rinse. This removes sunscreen and makeup.
  2. Foam or gel cleanser (폼클렌저) to remove sweat and the oil residue.

In the morning one gentle cleanser is enough. If your skin feels tight after washing, pick a low-pH or "mild" foam; Korean reviews often tag these as Gentle or Not drying.
