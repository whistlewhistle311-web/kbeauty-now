Korean skincare routine: the order that actually matters

Forget the 10-step myth. Most Koreans use four or five steps. Here is the order, what each step does, and the product Korean Hwahae users rank #1 for each step today.

1. Cleanse

Oil or balm at night to remove sunscreen, then a foam or gel cleanser. In the morning one gentle cleanser is enough.

Optional extras

Sheet masks once or twice a week after toner. Eye cream before moisturizer. Exfoliating pads or acids two or three nights a week, never on the same night as retinol.
