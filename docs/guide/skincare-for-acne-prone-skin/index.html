Korean skincare for acne-prone skin: what works, and what Koreans pick

Hwahae lets users register their skin type, so its rankings show what people with acne-prone skin actually rate highly. Here is how to build a routine for acne-prone skin, and today's top 5.

How to build the routine

Gentle cleansing twice a day, a BHA or heartleaf toner, and light non-comedogenic moisturizer. Do not skip sunscreen: it helps marks fade.

Ingredients to look for

BHA, heartleaf (어성초), tea tree, niacinamide, azelaic acid.

What to avoid

Over-washing, scrubs, and heavy oils on breakout areas.
