Best Korean sunscreen: what Koreans pick, and how to choose

Korean sunscreens are known for light textures and high protection. Here is how to read the labels, and the five sunscreens Korean Hwahae users rank highest today.

SPF50+ and PA++++

SPF measures protection from UVB (burning). PA measures protection from UVA (ageing and darkening); PA++++ is the highest grade on Korean labels. Most top Korean sunscreens are SPF50+ PA++++.

Mineral or chemical?

Korean labels call mineral (physical) sunscreens 무기자차 and chemical ones 유기자차. Mineral filters (zinc oxide, titanium dioxide) suit sensitive skin but can leave a white cast. Chemical filters feel lighter. Look for the review tag "No white cast" if that matters to you.
